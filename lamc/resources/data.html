Next generation sequencing of pea (Pisum sativum) genome

Data related to the paper Macas et al. (2007) BMC Genomics 8:427.

  • 454 reads and corresponding quality scores
  • Assembled contig sequences
  • BLAST search of the 454 reads or contigs assembled from 454 reads

Next generation sequencing of Silene latifolia genome

Data related to the paper Macas et al. (2011) PLoS ONE 6: e27335.

  • Pre-processed 454 reads from male and female genome
  • Pre-processed Illumina reads from male and female genome
  • Contigs assembled from 454 reads
  • Output of the 454 read clustering and repeat characterization analysis (multiple directories/files, tar.gz compressed, 361 MB)
  • Repeat-specific BLAST databases of 454 reads (multiple files, tar.gz compressed). The databases can also be
    searched via PROFREP server.

PlantSat database

A compilation of known plant satellite repeats (Macas et al. 2002). It can be accessed here.
